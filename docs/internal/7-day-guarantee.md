# 7-day money-back guarantee (internal)

Approved July 17, 2026.

Public policy: full refund within 7 days of first subscription purchase. Significant usage is reviewed case by case and may net out consumed credits. First subscription purchase only; renewals and top-up packs are excluded.

Admin-only fair-use review:
- Clean-refund threshold: 25 or fewer light tasks and 5 or fewer heavy credits consumed inside the first 7 days from initial charge timestamp.
- Above threshold: manual case-by-case review; refund may net out consumed heavy credits at the top-up rate ($37 / 25 credits).
- Week-one heavy-credit cap: Starter 3, Pro 8, Operator 15. Light tasks are not capped.
- Admins can lift the week-one heavy cap for an individual account from Dashboard → Admin Dashboard → 7-day Guarantee Refund Review.

Do not publish the internal thresholds in customer-facing copy.
