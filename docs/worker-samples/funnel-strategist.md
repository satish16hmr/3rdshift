## Stage 2: Landing Page

**Goal:** Convert cold traffic into free trial signups. One job. Nothing else.

**Page Structure**

**Headline**
"See Exactly Why Users Churn — Before They Do"

**Subheadline**
"Acme Analytics gives SaaS founders a real-time view of churn risk, activation gaps, and revenue health. Free trial. No credit card required."

**Above the Fold CTA**
Single email field + "Start My Free Trial" button. That's it.

**Social Proof Block**
- 3 founder quotes focused on specific outcomes ("Spotted a churn spike 3 weeks early and saved $12K MRR")
- Logos of recognizable SaaS companies using the product
- A single trust stat: "Trusted by 1,200+ SaaS founders"

**Feature Section**
Lead with outcomes, not features.
- ❌ "Real-time event tracking"
- ✅ "Know the exact moment users stop engaging — and why"

**Friction Reducers**
- "No credit card required" repeated near every CTA
- "Setup takes under 15 minutes"
- "Cancel anytime"

**Second CTA**
Bottom of page. Same signup form. Different headline: "Ready to stop guessing? Start free today."

**What to Remove**
Navigation menu. External links. Pricing page links. Anything that pulls attention away from the signup.

## Stage 3: Free Trial Signup

**Goal:** Get the signup with as little friction as possible.

**Signup Form Rules**
- Collect: Email + Company Name only
- No phone number. No credit card. No "how did you hear about us."
- Confirm immediately with a branded welcome screen: "You're in. Check your inbox to activate your account."

**Retargeting Setup**
Install a pixel on the landing page immediately. Anyone who visits but doesn't sign up goes into a retargeting audience. Run LinkedIn and Google retargeting ads with a softer angle — a case study or a "still thinking about it?" message.

---

## Stage 4: Onboarding Email Sequence

**Goal:** Turn signups into activated users. An unactivated trial user is a lost conversion.

**Define Activation First**
Pick one action that signals a user "gets it." For Acme Analytics, that's likely: *connected their data source + viewed their first dashboard.*

**Email Sequence**

---

**Email 1 — Immediately After Signup**
*Subject:* "Your Acme account is ready — here's where to start"

Get them to one action only. Not five. Not a feature tour. One thing: connect your data source. Include a 90-second video walkthrough. Link directly to the setup step.

---

**Email 2 — Day 2 (if not yet activated)**
*Subject:* "Takes 4 minutes. Shows you everything."

Address the stall. Most founders get busy. This email is a gentle nudge with a direct link back to the setup step. Add one founder testimonial about the "aha moment."

---

**Email 3 — Day 4 (activated users only)**
*Subject:* "What are your numbers telling you?"

Now that they're in, push them deeper. Highlight a feature they probably haven't discovered — churn prediction, cohort analysis, whatever's most sticky. Frame it around a question they're already asking themselves.

---

**Email 4 — Day 7 (all trial users)**
*Subject:* "One week in — here's what founders usually find"

Social proof-heavy. Share a short case study or data point. Remind them how much trial time is left. Soft CTA to upgrade.

---

**Email 5 — Day 12 (non-activated users)**
*Subject:* "Want us to just set it up for you?"

Offer a 20-minute live setup call. Remove the barrier entirely. Some founders will take this. Those who do convert at a much higher rate.

---

**Email 6 — Day 13 (all trial users)**
*Subject:* "Your trial ends tomorrow"

Direct. Clear. Here's what you'll lose access to. Here's how to keep it. One button: Upgrade Now.

---

## Stage 5: Trial-to-Paid Conversion

**Goal:** Convert active trial users before or at the moment the trial ends.

**In-App Nudges**
These matter as much as email. Build them in.

- **Usage milestone trigger:** When a user views their 5th dashboard, show a modal — "You're getting the full picture. Don't lose access when your trial ends." + upgrade CTA
- **Churn risk trigger:** If a user hasn't logged in for 3 days, send an automated in-app message: "Pick up where you left off" with a direct link to their last dashboard
- **Trial expiry banner:** Appears on day 10. Persistent but not intrusive. Shows days remaining.

**Upgrade Page**
When they click any upgrade CTA, send them to a dedicated upgrade page — not the public pricing page. This page should:
- Recap what they've already done in the trial ("You've tracked 3 data sources and caught 2 churn signals")
- Show exactly what they keep by upgrading
- Offer a single recommended plan (don't make them choose between five options)
- Include a one-click annual discount: "Save 20% — pay annually"

**Sales Assist Trigger**
Any trial user who logs in 5+ times but hasn't upgraded by day 11 gets flagged for a personal outreach. One founder-to-founder email from your CEO or a sales rep. Short, direct, no template feel.
