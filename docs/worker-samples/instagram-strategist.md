# Instagram Strategy for B2B Analytics SaaS

## The Core Problem First

SaaS founders are on Instagram. But they're not there to learn about software. They're there to decompress, get inspired, and occasionally get smarter. Your content has to meet them where they are — not where you wish they were.

That means less product, more perspective.

---

## Content Mix (3x/Week)

**Breakdown by week:**
- 1 Reel
- 1 Carousel
- 1 Single image or quote post

Rotate the themes below across that structure.

---

## Reel Concepts

**"The number that changed how we think about X"**
Hook with a surprising data point in the first 2 seconds. No fluff. Founders scroll fast. Example: *"Most SaaS companies measure the wrong churn metric. Here's what actually matters."* Keep it under 60 seconds. Talk to camera or use text-on-screen with good pacing.

**Myth-busting formats work well here**
*"Everyone says track MRR. We tracked this instead."* Short, punchy, ends with a clear takeaway.

**Behind the data**
Show a real dashboard moment (anonymized). Walk through what you noticed and what decision it led to. This builds credibility fast.

---

## Carousel Ideas

Carousels still get strong reach when they make people swipe. The key is a first slide that creates tension or curiosity.

**"5 metrics SaaS founders ignore until it's too late"**
Each slide = one metric. Simple visuals. Last slide = what to do about it.

**"We analyzed 50 SaaS dashboards. Here's what we found."**
Original data performs exceptionally well on Instagram right now. If you have aggregated, anonymized insights — use them. This is a trust-builder and a share magnet.

**"Before and after: what good data visibility actually looks like"**
Not a product demo. A concept demo. Show messy decisions vs. informed ones.

**Month-in-review carousels**
"3 things the data told us this month." Positions you as a thinking company, not just a selling company.

---

## Caption Approach

Keep the first line strong. Instagram cuts captions off early, so that first sentence has to earn the tap.

Good patterns:
- Ask a question that stings a little (*"Do you actually know why users are churning?"*)
- Make a statement that feels slightly controversial (*"Vanity metrics are keeping your team busy and your business blind."*)
- Start with a number (*"67% of the SaaS founders we surveyed couldn't answer this one question about their data."*)

Then deliver value in 3-5 short paragraphs. End with a simple, low-friction CTA. Not "buy now." More like "What metric do you check first every Monday? Drop it below."

## What the Algorithm Rewards Right Now

- **Reels with strong watch-through rate.** Front-load the value. Don't save the good stuff for the end.
- **Comments over likes.** Design content that provokes a response. Disagreement is fine. Silence isn't.
- **Shares and saves.** Carousels with genuinely useful information get saved. That signal matters a lot.
- **Consistency over volume.** Three quality posts a week beats seven mediocre ones every time.

---

## What to Avoid

Don't post product screenshots without context. Nobody cares about your UI unless you've already made them care about the problem it solves.

Don't write captions like press releases. Write like a smart founder talking to another smart founder.

Don't use stock photos of people pointing at whiteboards. It reads as fake immediately.

---

## First Month Focus

Spend the first month establishing a clear point of view. Pick one or two opinions about how SaaS founders should think about data and repeat them in different formats. Consistency of message builds recognition faster than variety does.

Track saves and shares more than likes. Those tell you what's actually resonating.
