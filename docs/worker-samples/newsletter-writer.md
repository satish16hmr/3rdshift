You spent real money getting that customer. Losing them quietly in month three is the most expensive thing your business does.

---

**RETENTION TIP 1: Send the "Are you still with us?" email at day 21**

Most SaaS companies celebrate the signup and then go silent.

Day 21 is when buyers feel buyer's remorse. They signed up, poked around, and maybe never got the "aha" moment. If you are not reaching out by then, someone else's pricing page is filling that silence.

Send a short, plain-text email from a real person. Ask one question: "What is the one thing you hoped [Product] would solve for you?" That reply is gold. It tells you exactly where your onboarding is failing.

---

**RETENTION TIP 2: Make your power users visible to everyone else**

People stay where they see success happening.

Find 3 to 5 customers who are getting real results and ask them to share a quick win. A short quote, a one-paragraph story, a before-and-after number. Feature that in your next email or inside the product dashboard if you can.

This is not a marketing tactic. It is a psychological anchor. It reminds struggling users that the outcome they wanted is actually possible here.

---

**RETENTION TIP 3: Build a "warning list" before they cancel**

The cancel button should never be a surprise to you.

Pull a list every Monday of users who have not logged in for 14 days, have not completed a core action, or have downgraded their usage. These are your at-risk accounts. Assign someone to personally reach out to five of them each week.

Not with a discount. With a genuine check-in.

"Hey, noticed you have not been in the platform lately. Anything we can help you figure out?" That sentence has saved more accounts than any winback campaign ever will.

---

**THE BIGGER PICTURE**

Retention is not about tricks. It is about closing the gap between what you promised and what people actually experience inside your product.

If your onboarding is confusing, fix the onboarding. If your support is slow, speed up the support. Tactics buy you time. The product keeps them.

But you need the time first. These three moves buy you that time.
