When someone does try to cancel, most SaaS companies either make it too hard or too easy.

The right approach is a smart offboarding flow.

When someone clicks cancel, before they confirm, show them three things:

**Three — A reason field.** Ask them why they're leaving. Not to guilt them. To learn. The answers here are gold. If 40 percent of people say "too expensive," that's a pricing conversation. If they say "missing a feature," that's a roadmap conversation.
