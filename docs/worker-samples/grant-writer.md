## Organization Overview

TechNonprofit Inc. is a nonprofit organization dedicated to closing the technology skills gap for underserved young people. We believe zip code should not determine a child's access to technical education or economic opportunity. Through direct programming and community partnerships, we connect youth to the skills, mentorship, and pathways they need to participate in the modern economy.

---

## Problem Statement

Millions of young people in low-income communities are growing up without access to computer science education. Public schools in underserved areas are significantly less likely to offer coding courses. Private alternatives cost hundreds to thousands of dollars — far beyond reach for most families. The result is a widening gap: technology jobs are among the fastest-growing and highest-paying in the country, yet the young people who most need economic mobility are the least likely to enter that pipeline.

This is not a talent problem. It is an access problem.

## Proposed Project

**Project Name:** Open Code — Free Coding Education Platform for Underserved Youth

**Grant Period:** 12 months

**Requested Funding:** $25,000

TechNonprofit Inc. will design and launch a fully free, publicly accessible online coding education platform built specifically for youth in underserved communities. The platform will require no account fees, no device purchases, and no prior experience.

### What We Will Build

- **Structured learning tracks** in foundational coding languages including HTML/CSS, JavaScript, and Python, sequenced for beginners ages 10–18
- **Project-based curriculum** that connects coding skills to real problems students care about — from building apps that serve their neighborhoods to designing tools for local organizations
- **Multilingual support** with content available in English and Spanish at launch
- **Offline accessibility** so students with limited or unreliable internet can download and complete lessons
- **Mentor connection features** linking students to volunteer industry professionals for guidance and encouragement

### Who We Will Reach

In year one, we project the platform will serve **500 youth** across our target communities, with measurable growth in subsequent years as the platform scales at no additional infrastructure cost.
