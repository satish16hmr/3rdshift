## Moderation Guidelines

**Response time standard:** Acknowledge every new question in #ask-the-community within 4 hours during business days.

**What to remove immediately:** Spam, unsolicited sales pitches, anything that demeans another member.

**What to redirect, not remove:** Self-promotion that's borderline — reply publicly and redirect to the right channel.

**What to celebrate publicly:** First-time posters, honest vulnerability, actionable advice that helps others.

**Tone standard:** Direct, warm, no corporate language. Write like a person, not a brand.
