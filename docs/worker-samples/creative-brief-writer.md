## Project Overview

Acme Analytics is launching a new churn prediction feature. This campaign needs to drive 500 free trial signups within 30 days of launch.

---

## The Problem We're Solving

SaaS founders lose customers quietly. By the time they notice the pattern, it's too late. This feature spots at-risk customers before they cancel — giving founders time to actually do something about it.

---

## Objective

**Primary:** 500 free trial signups in 30 days
**Secondary:** Establish Acme Analytics as the go-to analytics tool for SaaS retention

---

## Audience

**Who they are:** SaaS founders, typically running companies between $500K–$5M ARR. They wear a lot of hats. They care deeply about metrics but don't always have time to dig into dashboards. Churn keeps them up at night — especially if they've been burned by unexpected revenue drops before.

**What they believe:** They think they already have a handle on churn. Most don't. They're skeptical of tools that promise magic. They respond to specifics, not superlatives.

**What will move them:** A concrete number. A real use case. The feeling that someone finally built this the right way.

---

**Does not sound like:** An enterprise software brochure.

Know which customers are about to leave — before they do.
